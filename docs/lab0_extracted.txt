3.  Target  Users  
Roles  to  be  Implemented:  
1.  Student  (Buyer/Seller)  ○  Register/Login  using  college  credentials.  ○  List  products  for  auction.  ○  Browse  and  search  available  products.  ○  Place  bids  on  products.  ○  View  auction  status  and  bid  history.  ○  Manage  their  own  listings.  2.  Administrator  ○  Verify  and  manage  user  accounts.  ○  Approve  or  remove  inappropriate  product  listings.  ○  Monitor  ongoing  auctions.  ○  Resolve  disputes  and  manage  reported  listings.  ○  View  platform  statistics  and  activity.

6.  Navigation  Flow  
 
The  application  begins  with  the  Login/Register  page,  where  students  can  either  create  a  new  
account
 
or
 
log
 
in
 
using
 
their
 
credentials.
 
After
 
successful
 
authentication,
 
users
 
are
 
redirected
 
to
 
the
 
Home
 
Dashboard
,
 
which
 
displays
 
all
 
active
 
auction
 
listings.
 
From  the  Home  Dashboard ,  users  can:  
●  Open  the  Product  Details  page  to  view  complete  information  about  a  product  and  place  a  
bid.
 ●  Navigate  to  the  Add  Product  page  to  create  a  new  auction  listing  by  uploading  product  
details
 
and
 
images.
 ●  Access  the  My  Listings  page  to  view,  edit,  or  delete  their  own  auction  listings.  ●  Open  the  My  Bids  page  to  track  auctions  they  have  participated  in  and  monitor  their  bid  
status.
 ●  Visit  the  Profile  page  to  view  and  update  their  personal  information.  ●  Log  out  of  the  application.  
Administrators  log  in  through  the  same  authentication  page  but  are  redirected  to  the  Admin  
Dashboard
 
after
 
successful
 
login.
 
From
 
there,
 
they
 
can:
 
●  Manage  user  accounts.  ●  Approve,  edit,  or  remove  product  listings.  ●  Monitor  ongoing  auctions.
